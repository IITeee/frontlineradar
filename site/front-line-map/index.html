Front-line map of Ukraine

Which areas Russia and Ukraine control, according to the open-source mapping projects that track it daily.

Estimates, not real-time intelligence. Everything here comes from official statements and open-source (OSINT) reporting, is delayed by hours to days, and is attributed to its source. Figures are reported claims and may be revised.
No live military positions. This site does not publish unit locations, troop movements or anything that could help target people. It only links to published reporting and aggregates official statistics after the fact.
