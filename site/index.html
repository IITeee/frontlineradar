FAQ

What is Frontlineradar?

Frontlineradar is a free daily radar of the Russia–Ukraine war. It collects headlines from Ukrainian and international outlets, links the latest Institute for the Study of War (ISW) assessment, and charts Russian drone and missile attacks reported by Ukraine's Air Force. It is rebuilt every six hours.

Does Frontlineradar show real-time troop positions?

No. The site never shows live unit locations or troop movements. It only aggregates official statistics and links to published reporting, which is delayed by hours or days.

Where does the drone and missile data come from?

Daily launches and interceptions come from the open dataset 'Massive Missile Attacks on Ukraine' by Petro Ivaniuk, compiled from Ukrainian Air Force reports and licensed CC BY-NC-SA 4.0. Frontlineradar only publishes daily and weekly totals.

Who controls which areas of Ukraine?

The front-line map draws area-level territorial control from VIINA 2.0 (Zhukov & Ayers), licensed under the Open Database License (ODbL). Populated-place assessments are merged and simplified. The layer is delayed by days and approximate. It does not show military units or movements.

How is the daily brief written?

The daily brief is an automated digest. It groups the latest headlines by keyword into topics such as strikes, front line, energy and diplomacy, and adds the weekly strike numbers. It is not edited by a human and links to the original articles.

How often is the site updated?

Every six hours. Each page shows the time of the latest snapshot in UTC.

Sources and method

Refreshed every six hours by GitHub Actions. If a source fails, its last good data is kept and flagged below.
